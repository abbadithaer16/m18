The atelier

Slow
Metal

Every piece begins as a clay study worked over months, not days. We look for the moment a horse shifts its weight, the instant before movement, and hold it.

Bronze keeps that moment for centuries. It darkens, warms under the hand and takes on the room it lives in. We make objects meant to be inherited.
